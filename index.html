Who I Am

Fresh eyes on old problems.

I'm a Marine Science graduate from Universitas Padjajaran, Bandung — one of the class of 2022 who refused to stay in the classroom. My work sits at the intersection of physical oceanography, climate variability, and what those things actually mean for communities living along Indonesia's coastlines.

I've collected water quality data in Sukabumi and Lampung, studied climate change at ITB, attended NUS Singapore's fellowship on generative AI, and published eleven peer-reviewed papers — two in Scopus Q2 international journals — most before finishing my undergraduate degree. I think the best researchers know how to be in the field, in the lab, and at the policy table.
